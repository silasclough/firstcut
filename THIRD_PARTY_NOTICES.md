## Online services (only when the person uses them)

- **Openverse** (music and photos): each item carries its own Creative Commons licence; FirstCut keeps only CC0, Public Domain
  Mark and CC BY, and writes the required credit into the project's Notes and next to every download.
- **Pexels** and **Pixabay** (stock video and photos): used under the Pexels License and the Pixabay Content License with the
  person's own free API keys, kept in the macOS Keychain.
- **Epidemic Sound**: search and previews with the person's own API key; downloads need a licence from Epidemic Sound.
- **Claude** and **ChatGPT** accounts: the person's own plan, signed in through each company's official sign-in; their terms apply.

## Assets

FirstCut ships no third-party fonts, icons, music or footage. Graphics use the fonts installed with macOS. The small animated
accents (arrow, check, underline, ping) are drawn by FirstCut's own code. The app icon is generated by `app/VideoBuilder/icon.py`.
